Every atom carries a question no instrument has answered yet,  
Every bond a quiet argument no theory has settled yet.

I mapped the kinase domain at midnight, residue by residue,  
Searching for the pocket where the unknown has settled yet.

The simulation runs for a hundred nanoseconds of silence,  
And still the protein folds into a shape not settled yet.

They say the helix is stable, the beta-sheet resolved,  
But the loop between them trembles — nothing settled yet.

What the docking score predicts, the cell may contradict,  
Biology has arguments that numbers haven't settled yet.

I write equations by day and ghazals by night,  
Two languages for one truth not settled yet.
